PAIA Manual

Manual published in terms of section 51 of the Promotion of Access to Information Act 2 of 2000 (“PAIA”), read with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (“POPIA”).
FourX Games Proprietary Limited · Last updated 10 August 2026

1. About this manual

PAIA gives everyone a right of access to records held by private bodies where the record is required to exercise or protect a right. This manual explains what records FourX Games holds, and how to ask for one. It is available free of charge on this page, and on request by email.

4. Records available without a formal request

These are published and need no PAIA request:

  • Our Privacy Policy and Terms and Conditions.
  • Instructions for deleting your account and the data that goes with it — Delete your account.
  • Your own player profile and progression, which you can view in the game at any time.
  • This manual.

If you want a copy of your own personal information, that is a POPIA access request rather than a PAIA request, and the quickest route is to email the address above — see section 6 of the Privacy Policy. You do not need to complete a PAIA form for it.

7. How to request a record

  1. Complete the prescribed PAIA request form (Form 2, available from the Information Regulator's website) and email it to privacy@fourxgames.com.
  2. Identify the record clearly, state which right you are seeking to exercise or protect, and explain how the record will help you do so.
  3. Tell us the form in which you would like it and how to reach you.
  4. We will respond within 30 days. That period may be extended by a further 30 days where the request is for a large number of records or requires a search through records held elsewhere; we will tell you if that happens, and why.

Fees

Requests for your own personal information carry no fee. For other requests, the fees prescribed under PAIA apply: a request fee payable before the request is processed, and an access fee covering search, preparation and reproduction. We will give you a written estimate before doing any work you would be charged for, and you may withdraw the request rather than pay it.

If we refuse

We will give reasons in writing. You may then lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator, or apply to a court. PAIA also allows us to refuse a request in defined circumstances — for example where the record contains another person's personal information, is legally privileged, or would disclose commercially confidential information.

8. Complaints

Complaints about how we handle personal information or access requests can be made to us at privacy@fourxgames.com, and to the Information Regulator at POPIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za (POPIA) or PAIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za (PAIA).
